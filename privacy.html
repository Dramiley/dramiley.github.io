Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Controller and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Robin Morgenstern, operating as dramiley ("we", "us") processes personal data in "HideZone: GPS Hide & Seek" and on this website. Contact: dramileydev@gmail.com.

HideZone does not require an email address or social login. However, the app uses anonymous identifiers and processes the data necessary to provide live GPS gameplay, advertising, diagnostics and subscriptions.

2. Data We Collect and Process

Depending on how you use HideZone, we process the following categories:

  • Player and lobby data: Player name, anonymous Firebase user ID, Zone ID, team, role, host status, game settings and match events.
  • Precise location: GPS coordinates, accuracy and timestamps during an active match, including while the app is in the background or the screen is locked.
  • Chat: Messages sent to other players in the same private lobby.
  • Device and other identifiers: Firebase installation and Crashlytics identifiers, advertising ID or IDFA where available, SDK identifiers and consent information.
  • App activity and analytics: Feature interactions, game mode selections, session events and aggregated usage information.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs, stack traces, device model, operating system, app version, performance and network state.
  • Advertising data: Ad impressions, clicks, consent status, advertising identifiers and approximate location derived from the IP address where permitted.
  • Subscription data: RevenueCat anonymous App User ID, product, entitlement status, transaction and receipt information. We do not receive full payment card details.

3. Purposes and Legal Bases

We process player names, anonymous IDs, location, chat and game state to provide the game under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Security and essential diagnostics are processed to provide a reliable service and on the basis of our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Analytics and advertising are based on consent where required. Subscription records are processed to perform the subscription agreement and meet legal obligations.

Location access is necessary for live GPS matches. If you deny it, you cannot participate in a live round. Chat is optional unless a particular game interaction requires it.

4. Retention and Deletion

  • Live match data: Location, chat and game state are deleted when a game is properly ended. Automated cleanup periodically removes abandoned or interrupted lobby records, so deletion may occur later. We do not create permanent movement profiles or chat archives.
  • Anonymous Firebase identifiers: Authentication and installation identifiers may remain after a game until deleted, reset by the service or removed following a verified request.
  • Firebase Analytics: User-level event data is retained for no longer than 14 months; aggregated reports may remain longer. The configured Firebase setting may shorten this period.
  • Crashlytics: Crash and diagnostic data is generally retained for up to 90 days.
  • Subscriptions: RevenueCat and the app stores retain transaction and entitlement data as necessary for subscription management, restoration, fraud prevention, accounting and legal duties.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

Your player name, role, game actions, chat messages and game-related position may be visible to players in the same private lobby. Only join lobbies with people you trust and do not use your full legal name as a player name.

We use the following providers:

  • Google Firebase: Anonymous Authentication, Realtime Database, Analytics and Crashlytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Advertising, measurement and consent management in eligible lobbies. Google advertising information
  • RevenueCat: Subscription validation and entitlement management. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Play and Apple App Store: Payment processing and subscription management under their own privacy terms.

We do not sell personal data. Providers may process data in the United States or other countries. Where the GDPR applies, transfers rely on an adequacy decision, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable, Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful safeguard offered by the provider.

6. App Permissions and Choices

The app requests the following permissions for the described purposes:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION / iOS Location Always: Required to calculate your distance to zones, track active position during gameplay, and maintain live game balance even when the device is locked or the app is minimized during a match.
  • INTERNET & FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Required to maintain connection to Firebase servers and run the location service during live matches.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to alert you when game rounds start, zone shrink countdowns trigger, or ping reveals occur.
  • App Tracking Transparency: Requested on iOS for advertising purposes where applicable. Denying tracking does not block core gameplay.

You can revoke permissions in your device settings and revisit available advertising privacy choices in the app. Revoking a permission may disable the corresponding feature.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of personal data, object to processing and withdraw consent for future processing. You may also complain to a competent data protection authority.

To make a request, email dramileydev@gmail.com. Include your platform and any anonymous Firebase or RevenueCat identifier available to you. We may request limited information to verify that a request relates to you. Do not email precise location histories, passwords, full payment card details or unredacted store receipts unless specifically requested.

8. Users Aged 13-17

HideZone is not intended for children under 13. A user who is at least 13 but has not reached the age at which they can independently agree to these terms or relevant data processing in their country must use HideZone only with permission from a parent or legal guardian. Parents and guardians should review live location sharing, private lobby membership and chat risks and supervise the choice of play area.

Do not share a home address, school, phone number, full legal name or other sensitive information in a player name or lobby chat.

9. Website, Security and Changes

This website itself does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The hosting infrastructure necessarily receives technical request data such as IP address, request time and browser information to deliver and protect the site. The main and download pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which causes the browser to connect to Google and transmit technical connection data such as the IP address. Links to app stores and provider policies lead to third-party websites.

We use encrypted connections and provider access controls, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy when features, providers or legal requirements change.
